# G1 bill of materials: sheet 01 Power Tree (1xx) and sheet 07 Power Stage (7xx, power board)

P3 part selection, 2026-11-17. Basis: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (decisions D-01..D-16 decided, D-17+ taken as the recommended value and marked), `docs/architecture/B_power_tree.md`, `docs/architecture/A_power_stage.md`, `instruction.md` s.3.3 and s.5. No project file was edited.

**Sources.** Every LCSC number below was read live this session from the **LCSC product API** (`wmsc.lcsc.com/ftps/wm/product/detail`, MPN, manufacturer, LCSC stock, price ladder, datasheet link) **and** the **JLCPCB parts API** (`jlcpcb.com/api/overseas-pcb-order/v1/shoppingCart/smtGood/selectSmtComponentList`, JLC stock and Basic / Extended / preferred flag). `jlcsearch.tscircuit.com` was used only for discovery (its stock field is currently null). Datasheets were downloaded from the LCSC datasheet link or ti.com and read as text (pypdf / pypdfium2); drawing-only PDFs were rendered and read as images. Page numbers are PDF pages.

**Conventions.** `Fit` = assembled; `DNP` = footprint with full purchasing data, not fitted; `MECH` = purchased non-PCBA part (hand-assembled); `NOBOM` = copper-only footprint (`in_bom no`). Stock = `JLC n / LCSC m`. Unit price = LCSC ladder at qty x 100 boards (est.). Brand labels differ between the two APIs for the same part (e.g. LCSC "R+O" = JLC "hongjiacheng", "TI" = "Texas Instruments"); the MPN matched exactly on both for every line.

## 4. UNVERIFIED items

No LCSC number in this BOM is unverified. The following ratings or facts could not be confirmed this session:

1. **R101 / R102 (HoLRT 2512 1 Ohm 2 W) sub-ms single-pulse rating.** The fault energy is 0.53 J per resistor (calc from the fuse melting I2t of 0.53 A2s at 29 A, about 0.6 ms). That is far below the 5x-rated-power 5 s overload (50 J, DS p.7), but no single-pulse curve is published. Bench-check it, or use a pulse-rated part.
2. **XFCN T68160200-1400:** the 200 A basis (no temperature rise stated) and the **2.5 N.m thread torque** (drawing p.1). That torque is low for an M6 cable lug at 200 A (typically 4-6 N.m; that range is not from a document).
3. **IPT015N10N5 SOA** at 80-100 V for 15-20 us. It is a graph and was not read numerically (carried over from A).
4. **Shunt alternatives.**
   * C469442 (Yezhan ASR-M-7-0.2F): the datasheet is image-only, so TCR and power come from the listing.
   * C558625 (Milliohm HolRS3920): +-50 ppm and 5 W were read from DS p.3 and p.5, but the 3920 footprint and thermal path are not designed.
5. **DC-bias derating** of the 250 V 2220 (PSA) and 25 V 1210 (Samwha) MLCCs: the curves were not read, so the percentages above are estimates.
6. **B0505S-2WR3 below its 40 mA minimum load** (standby is about 15-20 mA). The output may exceed the 5.5 V transceiver limit (B s.3.6). The R122 dummy load stays DNP until the bring-up measurement.
7. **R735 / R737 alternative** (Arcol HS100 50R or equivalent) is only a suggestion; it was not checked at any distributor this session.
8. **Non-LCSC mechanical lines** MECH707-MECH710 (Cu bars, gap pad, bolts, M4 kit) have no distributor number by design. Quantities and lengths are estimates from A s.4.6 / s.10.3.
9. **Footprints that need an import and a pad check:**
   * the WSLP5931 shunt has no KiCad footprint: `easyeda2kicad --lcsc_id=C413492`, then add Kelvin pads;
   * the XFCN terminal: `easyeda2kicad --lcsc_id=C45384693`, 8 slotted legs 2.5 x 3.6.
10. **Stock footprints that still need a pad check:**

## 5. Design changes forced by the selection (owner / integrator to approve)

1. **All MLCCs on 58 V nodes go to 250 V.**
   * The 2x rule needs >= 117 V at 58.4 V. A and B used 100 V parts (C_IN, the BAT+/PACK+ clamp MLCC, the snubber C and the kill speed-up C).
   * Now: 2.2 uF 250 V 2220 (C153032) x 10, 100 nF 250 V 1206 (DNP snubbers), and 1 nF 2 kV 1206 (Basic).
   * Cost is about +$2.4 per board. The 2220 cases are larger; keep them away from bolt holes and board flex.
   * C_IN becomes 3 x 2.2 uF at U101 plus 1 x 2.2 uF at U102.
2. **F_SUP moves to the power board (F701, at the B+ lug)** instead of sheet 01.
   * Otherwise the tap trace and the B2B pin are unfused from an 8 kA source, next to GND_PWR pins on a 2.54 mm connector.
   * This needs a `VSUP_TAP` B2B pin. Use the HV pin freed by D-15, in the HV group, with a spare pin as a gap.
   * R_DAMP, D101 and D103 stay on sheet 01.
3. **Series blocking diodes in the pre-discharge and pre-charge paths (D711, D712, ES2D).** Without them the P-FET body diodes bypass the open banks:
   * Through Q724 + 100 Ohm, the pack discharges into a PACK+ short at 0.58 A / 34 W continuously (68 % of the 50 W part A chose), and it powers any load in Ship.
   * Through Q723 + 50 Ohm, a charger can still charge with CHG off.
   * TI's AN09 Fig 1-1 uses 1 kOhm in these paths, where the effect is negligible.
4. **LM5013-Q1 is non-synchronous** (DS p.13), so it needs an external Schottky: **D104 SS520-SMC** (200 V, 5 A, Tj 175 C).
   * SS5200C was rejected: its Tj max is 125 C (DS p.1), which leaves no margin.
   * New U101 values: **RON 39 k (321 kHz), FB 150 k / 47 k (5.03 V), RA 150 k 0805, CA 2.2 nF C0G, CB 100 pF, L 22 uH with Isat 7 A.** DS p.18 recommends >= 22 uH and Isat > 7 A for transients above 72 V.
5. **U102 LM5163-Q1 values:** RON 100 k (300 kHz), FB 220 k / 24 k (12.2 V), RA 470 k 0805, CA 2.2 nF C0G, CB 100 pF, L 100 uH with Isat 3.5 A.
6. **Power-latch values:** R_AUX 470 k -> **2 M** and R_FORCE 1 M -> **2 M**.
   * This keeps PWR_EN <= 19.5 V (it reached 39 V with AUX at 58 V), so C_EN can be a 50 V Basic part with 2.5x margin.
   * EN still crosses 1.6 V at AUX 9 V (2.8 V, calc).
   * The RTC_WAKE diode is omitted (no MCU pin, ARCHITECTURE s.10).
7. **TLV62569 output capacitance is limited to 10-47 uF** (DS p.10). B's "bulk >= 100 uF on +3V3_ESP" is therefore not allowed, and 2 x 22 uF is fitted. Any extra bulk must sit behind a separate filter, or the ESP rail must move to a part rated for it.
8. **Kill level shifter BSS123 -> BSS131 (240 V).** This removes the 0 % margin flagged in ARCHITECTURE s.6.
9. **TPS22918-Q1 -> TPS22918DBVR (not AEC),** because the -Q1 has only 114 pcs.
   * CT = 100 pF C0G gives a 0.43 ms ramp instead of about 1 ms.
   * A 330 pF C0G (Extended) would give 1.3 ms if the module needs it.
10. **The reverse-charge clamp (DNP) needs R718 changed when it is fitted.**
    * With Q725 on and PACK+ at -58 V, R718 = 100 Ohm would dissipate about 34 W.
    * AN09 uses 10 kOhm in that position plus a diode-steered fast path (D723 footprint).
    * R740 is raised from AN09's 10 k to 47 k, which keeps it at 30 % power.
11. **R_PCHG = HoRX-100W-100R:** the same family, mounting and wiring as R_PRE. PCHG is populated per D-23.
12. **Heater gate stage.**
    * 2N7002 (Basic) + MMBT5401 from +12V, giving VGS about 11.8 V.
    * The power board gets a 100 k HEATER_EN pull-down (R748), so an unplugged B2B keeps the heater off.
    * The FET changes to BSC098N10NS5.
13. **Pull-downs on the power board.**
    * The AFE_FLT_DSG_N / AFE_FLT_CHG_N 470 k pull-downs (D-25) sit on the power board (R732, R733) at U702, so an unplugged B2B reads as a fault. Sheets 02 / 08 must not add a second pair.
    * The FET_EN 100 k pull-down (R731) is also on the power board.
    * MCU_NRST gets no pull-down here: it would fight the MCU's internal pull-up.
14. **Stack height** = 8.5 mm socket + 2.5 mm header body = **11.0 mm** (A assumed 12-15 mm). The standoffs are M3 x 11 F-F (MECH701-706).
15. **Refs outside the sheet plan.**
    * The shunts (R742, R743), NTC_SHUNT (RT702) and the heater stage (Q726-Q728, R744-R748, D724, J710) are power-board parts that the sheet plan puts on sheets 04 / 05.
    * They are numbered 7xx here because the brief says the power board uses 7xx.
    * The integrator should confirm this or renumber them to 4xx / 5xx.
