# G2 BOM: sheet 02 AFE / Cell Monitor and sheet 04 Current Sense

P3 part selection, 2026-11-17. Scope: every resistor, capacitor, IC, transistor, diode, connector, ESD part and test point on sheet 02 (refs 2xx, control board) and sheet 04 (refs 4xx, power board, including the Kelvin filters at the shunt). Basis: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (D-01..D-16 decided), `docs/architecture/C_afe.md`, `docs/architecture/A_power_stage.md` sec.5, `instruction.md` sec.3.3 and sec.5. No project file was changed.

**Verification source.** Every LCSC number was queried live on 2026-11-17 through the **JLCPCB parts API** (`jlcpcb.com/api/overseas-pcb-order/v1/shoppingCart/smtGood/selectSmtComponentList`, exact `componentCode` match). MPN, Manufacturer, Stock (JLC) and Basic/Extended in the tables are copied from that record. `jlcsearch.tscircuit.com` was used only to find candidates; the LCSC product API (`wmsc.lcsc.com`) only to confirm old numbers that JLC does not list (C8677, C131010, C85073, C84296, C14666). Datasheets were downloaded from the LCSC datasheet links and read as text with pypdf; page numbers are PDF pages. The BQ76952 copy is the TI Chinese edition ZHCSM58B (Nov 2021), whose pagination matches SLUSE13B as cited in C.

**Sheet assignment.** ARCHITECTURE sec.3 is not ambiguous: U_LATCH1 (SN74AUP1G74) is on sheet 04; the gate-kill logic U_KILL1..3, kill FETs and the FET_EN / MCU_NRST pull-downs are on sheet 07 (not in this BOM). The control-board half of J_B2B is on sheet 02 (integrator proposal). The 100 k `AFE_DFETOFF` pull-down and the 470 k DDSG / DCHG pull-downs sit at the BQ pins on sheet 02. `OC_CLR`'s 100 k pull-down sits at the latch on sheet 04.

Extra columns after the playbook fields: `Qty`, `Fit` (FIT / DNP / NOT IN BOM). The CSV also has a `Sheet` column.

## Design-impacting findings

* **F-1 (high) The 100 nF on CLR_N slows the primary short-circuit trip by about 12 us.** The TLV1702 open-collector output has to discharge C405 from 3.3 V to the AUP VIL of 0.9 V with its 20 mA typical sink current (TLV1702-Q1 DS p.6; SN74AUP1G74 DS p.4): 100 nF x 2.4 V / 20 mA = 12 us (calc). ARCHITECTURE sec.5.6 budgets 0.03 us for that step. At 39 A/us the turn-off current rises from about 0.75 kA to about 1.2 kA (calc), which eats the FET and TVS margin of ARCHITECTURE sec.6. **Proposal (all Basic, no new Extended line):** keep R415 10 k on CLR_N, set C405 to DNP (or 100 pF), and add a separate power-on node: R 100 k (C25803) from +3V3 to a node with 100 nF (C14663) to GND, coupled to CLR_N through a 1N4148WS (C2128, anode on CLR_N). At power-up CLR_N sits at the diode drop (about 0.62 V at 0.33 mA, < 0.9 V) for about 7 ms; afterwards the diode is reverse-biased and the comparator only sees 10 k plus a few pF. BAT54WS (C124205, Extended) gives more margin if wanted.
* **F-2 C_BAT1 must be 250 V.** BAT sits at up to 58.4 V to VSS; the architecture's 1 uF 100 V (C13832) is 1.7x, below the 2x rule. C217 = 1 uF 250 V 1812 (C381468, 88 251 in stock, $0.19).
* **F-3 SRP / SRN filter placement conflicts with TI.** TI wants the 100 Ohm resistors and the 100 nF placed at the BQ, not at the shunt (BQ DS p.66, p.77). ARCHITECTURE puts 2 x 200 Ohm averaging resistors at the shunt (power board) and C places a further 100 Ohm R_SRP1 / R_SRN1 at the BQ: together that is 200 Ohm per leg and tau = 40 us, which delays SCD further. The BOM keeps the architecture's tau = 20 us by fitting R253 / R254 as 0 Ohm. **Recommended alternative (all Basic):** R403-R406 = 20 Ohm (C22950) at the shunt for averaging only, R253 / R254 = 100 Ohm (C22775) at the BQ; leg = 110 Ohm, tau = 22 us, and the filter sits at the device as TI asks.
* **F-4 The shunt TCR is +-225 ppm/C, not +-125.** WSLP DS p.2 lists component TCR +-225 ppm/C for the 0.2 mOhm WSLP5931 (+125 ppm is the 0.2 mOhm WSLP3921). TI asks for <= 50 ppm (BQ DS p.76). The uncompensated error at +35 K is about 0.8 % (calc), not 0.4-0.5 % (A sec.5.1, D-22). NTC_SHUNT compensation (RT401) becomes mandatory, not optional.
* **F-5 INA240A1QPWRQ1 costs $13.11 at JLC (100+), 1 893 in stock**, 53 % of the G2 cost. INA240A1PWR (C93965, non-Q, $2.03, 2 371 in stock) has the same PW pinout (non-Q DS p.3 vs Q1 DS p.4). The SOIC -Q1 (C2057377) has 0 stock. The v1 is not AEC-Q anyway (ARCHITECTURE sec.11 sec.1.1). Owner choice.
* **F-6 Balance-resistor derating at high ambient.** 0.38 W is 38 % of 1 W only up to 70 C; the UNI-ROYAL curve derates linearly to 0 at 155 C (DS p.5). With the 30-40 K bank rise (C sec.4.3, est.) at 85 C ambient the local temperature is about 120 C, where the rating is about 0.41 W: 93 % load. Options: firmware throttle on NTC_BAL (RT201) and Max Internal Temp (keep the bank below about 95 C local for <= 50 %), or the 2 W 2512 PS122WF330JT4E (C2793905, 3 042 in stock = about 190 boards, Extended, $0.083).
* **F-7 REG18 capacitor.** A 2.2 uF part can fall below the 1.8 uF minimum (BQ DS p.13) after -10 % tolerance and the X7R/X5R temperature swing (2.2 x 0.9 x 0.85 = 1.7 uF, calc). C220 = 4.7 uF X7R 16 V 0805 (C3039701). REG1 (>= 1 uF, DS p.14) keeps 2.2 uF X7R (C19110). Both X7R instead of the current Basic X5R C23630 (warm area near the balance bank).
* **F-8 Lettered-pin symbols in the current sheet 02.** The 16 Q_BL use `Device:Q_NMOS`, whose pin numbers are the letters D / G / S; they cannot map to SOT-23 pads 1 / 2 / 3. KiCad 10 `Device:Q_NPN` has the same problem (B / C / E). Use `Transistor_BJT:SS8050` (B=1, E=2, C=3, matching the SS8050 DS p.1 pinout 1 B, 2 E, 3 C), `Transistor_FET:2N7002` (G=1, S=2, D=3) and `Transistor_FET:IRLML0030`-style (G=1, S=2, D=3) for IRLML0100.
* **F-9 DFETOFF abs max resolved.** DFETOFF / DDSG / DCHG / ALERT / RST_SHUT abs max is VSS + 6 V and the ROC is 0-5.5 V (BQ DS p.7, p.9), so the 3.3 V AND-gate drive of `AFE_DFETOFF` is fine (ARCHITECTURE sec.15 item). LD / PACK abs max is 85 V (DS p.7), still below the 96.8 V rated clamp: R255 / R256 limit the current but the pin rating question stays open.
* **F-10 JST XH is rated -25 C** (JST DS p.1; HCTL XH listing the same), below the v1 rating of -40..85 C (D-14). Affects J202, J203, J204, J205. Verified Micro-Fit alternatives rated -40..+105 C: Molex 430450800 2 x 4 (C122422, 2 429 in stock, $0.44) for J202 and Molex 430450400 2 x 2 (C122413, 49 442, $0.29) for J204 / J203; KiCad footprints `Molex_Micro-Fit_3.0_43045-0800_2x04` / `-0400_2x02` exist. The HCTL Micro-Fit clone for J201 (C3294390) is listed -25 C at JLC but -40..+105 C in its own spec (p.4).
* **F-11 C_DF dielectric.** The house rule says C0G for filters. C201-C216 stay X7R (TI value, 3.65 V on 50 V = negligible DC-bias loss, the RC only needs 44 us << 3 ms). A C0G 220 nF is 1206 only (GRM31C5C1H224JE02L, C464977, 50 756 in stock, $0.28): +$4.4 per board and 16 large parts in the cell-input area. Owner to accept the deviation. All other filter caps (C223, C224, C227-C230, C401, C403) are C0G.
* **F-12 Q218 2N7002 for the balancer enable.** C allows the module to sink up to 100 mA; that is 87 % of the 2N7002 115 mA rating, with 7 Ohm at 5 V gate (DS p.1-2) and more at 3.3 V. Limit the module enable to <= 50 mA in the interface spec, or use AO3400A (C20917, Basic, 30 V) if the enable never sees more than 15 V.
* **F-13 Fail-safe with the B2B unplugged.** The 470 k DDSG / DCHG pull-downs and the 100 k DFETOFF pull-down sit on the control board (sheet 02). With J206 unplugged, U_KILL2's inputs on the power board float. Sheet 07 needs its own pull-downs on `AFE_FLT_DSG_N` / `AFE_FLT_CHG_N` (and `FET_EN`, `MCU_NRST`) as A sec.8.3 assumes. This is outside G2; reported for the sheet 07 owner.
* **F-14 The ARCHITECTURE parts list (sec.12) is stale on two numbers:** C_BAT1 (C13832, see F-2) and the B2B male header ("not found"): C56496 now has 7 173 in stock and is used for J206.
